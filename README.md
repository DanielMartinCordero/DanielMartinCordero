### How I work

- **Layers with one job each.** Models, services and screens are separated, so a change in one place does not ripple through the rest.
- **Secrets stay out of the repo.** Credentials and per-project Firebase files are git-ignored, and the data is protected with Firestore security rules.
- **Decisions and limits are written down.** My project READMEs explain why things are built the way they are and list the known limitations.

### [AI video generation pipeline](https://github.com/DanielMartinCordero/gen-video-orchestrator)

Python pipeline that turns a topic into a vertical short video. **Gemini** writes the script, **edge-tts** narrates every scene, **ComfyUI + Flux** (running on a local GPU) renders the images and **MoviePy** assembles the final MP4.
